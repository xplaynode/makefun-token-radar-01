How a launch is valued

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × seasoning × breadth

The category multiple is not a constant: it is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the category’s own tokenised protocols in this same snapshot, shrunk toward the global median by peer count, pulled further toward it when a category has fewer than three peers, and clamped to 0.02–2.00. Momentum reads the 7-day TVL change (capped −50% to +100%, worth at most ×1.50); seasoning reads how long DefiLlama has tracked the protocol with no token recorded (up to ×1.20 at four years); breadth adds 3% per extra chain, to ×1.21. Open any row to recompute its value factor by factor.

Set apart, never ranked as launches: wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade, and the live tokens themselves, shown at the market’s own values on their own tabs. The snapshot is DefiLlama’s record: a protocol here records no token; that is a statement about the record, not proof none exists.

Implied values are this radar’s own model claims, derived only from the snapshot’s figures; they are not predictions that a token will be issued. Farm plans are starting points: costs are estimates at typical fees, and a position in a pre-token protocol can lose money.
